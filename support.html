Need help, found a bug, or have a question or suggestion?

What the app does

Worldview Builder walks you through 47 political, economic, religious, and philosophical isms. You mark the ones you agree with, and the app builds a personal report comparing your choices against the author's own model from the book — including the book's own reasoning, strengths, and trade-offs for everything you've picked.

Frequently asked questions

I liked some isms, but the Worldview or Builder tab looks empty.

Liked isms are set from the Encyclopedia tab — tap an ism to read about it, then use the toggle switch on its row to like it. Worldview and Builder both update automatically from that same list.

Will I lose my choices if I close the app?

No — liked isms are saved on your device automatically and are still there the next time you open the app.

How do I get a copy of my report?

On the Builder tab, use the Export button to share or print your report, or Save PDF to save it directly as a file.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device — see the Privacy Policy for details.
