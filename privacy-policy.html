Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Worldview Builder is a companion app to the book A Critique of Isms by Gordon Anton. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Short version

Worldview Builder does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

What the app stores, and where

The only information the app remembers is which isms you've marked as “liked” while using the Encyclopedia. This is saved using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults), directly on your iPhone. It is never sent to any server, because the app doesn't have a server — there is no account, no sign-in, and no backend of any kind.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. Your usage of the app is not measured, logged, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Sharing and exporting reports

The Builder screen lets you export a PDF report of your choices and, optionally, share it using Apple's standard share sheet (for example, to save it, print it, or send it by email or AirDrop). That sharing happens entirely through Apple's own system tools and only when you actively choose to do it — the app itself does not transmit the report anywhere on its own.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve data leaving your device — this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to gordonanton@me.com.
